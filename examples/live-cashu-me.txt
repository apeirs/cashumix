# Notes
# - The checkstate burst (09:54:08 to 09:55:45) is cashu.me watching its pending 8 sat token
#   until cdk-cli spent it; the 09:56 WebSockets are its NUT-17 proof-state subscriptions.
#   /v1/mint/bolt11/batch is cashu.me's batched mint call. All passed through unchanged.
# - The 09:56:25 to 09:56:31 block is cdk-cli receiving the token (it fetches the keysets first).
# - The `cashumix checkstate` calls don't appear: the CLI talks to the mixnet itself. One of
#   them, before the receive, was cut off by a 120 s shell timeout while the mixnet was slow;
#   the one after the receive took 3.9 s.
# - Fees: testnut charges an input fee, so 8 sent arrive as 7 and the wallet pays 1 sat on
#   swaps and melts.
